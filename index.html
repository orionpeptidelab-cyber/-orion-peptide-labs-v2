Authenticity & Safety

Every batch tested.
Every certificate available.

We believe transparency is non-negotiable. That's why every product we dispatch undergoes independent third-party analysis before it ever reaches you.

🔬

Independent Lab Testing

All peptides are submitted to accredited third-party laboratories for HPLC analysis. We do not test in-house — independence is the point.

📋

Certificate of Analysis

Every batch ships with a full Certificate of Analysis (CoA) confirming compound identity, purity percentage, and absence of common contaminants.

✅

≥99% Purity Standard

We enforce a minimum 99% purity threshold. Any batch that fails to meet this standard is rejected — no exceptions, no compromises.

🧪

Sterility & Endotoxin Testing

Lyophilised peptides are tested for sterility and bacterial endotoxin levels to ensure they meet the standards expected of research compounds.

❄️

Cold-Chain Logistics

Peptides are temperature-sensitive. All products are stored at controlled temperatures and dispatched with appropriate cold-chain packaging.

🔒

Verified Sourcing

We work exclusively with verified manufacturers who operate under GMP guidelines and provide full traceability documentation.

✓ Third-Party Verified

Certificates of Analysis available on request

We maintain complete testing records for every batch ever dispatched. Request your product's CoA at any time by contacting our team.
